Plantar Fasciitis Embolization (PFE)

Plantar fasciitis is irritation of the plantar fascia, the thick band of tissue along the bottom of your foot. It often causes sharp heel pain with the first steps in the morning or after sitting.

Who May Benefit

Most plantar fasciitis gets better with stretching, supportive shoes, rest and other care. PFE may be an option if you:

  • Have had heel pain for 6 months or more
  • Have tried stretching, shoe inserts, therapy or injections without enough relief
  • Want to avoid surgery on the foot

How It Works

The catheter is placed in an artery at the top of the thigh or near the ankle. Contrast dye shows the small arteries around the heel with extra blood flow. Particles are released to reduce blood flow to the inflamed tissue.

Good to Know

PFE is a newer procedure. Early studies have been small, and more research is under way.
