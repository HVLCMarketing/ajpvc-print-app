Healthy Circulation Every Day

Keep walking

Walk most days. For PAD, walk until it’s moderately uncomfortable, rest, then walk again.

Don’t smoke

Quitting protects your arteries. Ask us about help to quit.

Know your numbers

Keep blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar in your target range.

Raise swollen legs

For vein swelling, raise your legs above heart level for short breaks. With PAD or foot pain at rest, ask us first.

Wear compression

If prescribed, put stockings on in the morning.

Check your feet

Look daily for cuts, color changes or sores, especially with diabetes.
