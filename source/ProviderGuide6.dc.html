Referring provider guide

Vein Care & DVT

Venous disease ranges from aching varicose veins to ulcers and clots. Most treatment happens in the office.

Venous Insufficiency

✓Symptomatic varicose veins, venous swelling or skin changes (CEAP C2 to C4)
✓Refer early: healed or active venous ulcers
✓Pathologic reflux: more than 0.5 s in superficial veins1
✓Early ablation healed ulcers faster and lowered the recurrence rate in EVRA2

DVT & Deep Veins

✓Same-day DVT rule-out for most insurances
✓Iliofemoral DVT or unexplained left-leg swelling
✓Post-thrombotic syndrome affects 20 to 50% of DVT patients3
✓Retrievable IVC filters: remove once PE protection is no longer needed4

Insurance Tip

Many plans require documented symptoms and a compression trial before vein ablation. Including the CEAP class and compression dates in your referral speeds approval.
