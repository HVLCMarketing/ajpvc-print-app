Arterial & venous guide

Your Procedure Day

Our team will give you instructions for your procedure. Follow them carefully and call us with any questions.

Before Your Procedure

Tell us about all your medicines. You may be asked to stop or adjust blood thinners or some diabetes medicines. Don’t stop any medicine unless we tell you to.
Tell us about allergies, especially to contrast (X-ray) dye or latex.
You may need blood tests to check kidney function and clotting.
Don’t eat or drink beforehand for as long as our staff tells you.
Arrange for an adult to drive you home.

On the Day

Wear loose, comfortable clothing. Bring your compression stockings if you have them.
Bring your ID, insurance card and medicine list.
An IV line may be placed for fluids and relaxing medicine.
After an artery procedure, you’ll rest for a few hours while the puncture site is checked.

After an Artery Procedure

  • Keep the puncture site clean and dry for 24 hours
  • Avoid heavy lifting for several days
  • Walk as advised; it helps circulation
  • Take antiplatelet medicine exactly as prescribed

After a Vein Procedure

  • Walk right away and often
  • Wear compression as directed
  • Expect some bruising and tightness
  • Go to your follow-up ultrasound

We Check Your Insurance First

We work to verify your benefits, including any compression stocking or referral requirements, and will explain expected costs before your procedure is scheduled.
