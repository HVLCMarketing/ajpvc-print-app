Joint pain program · Shoulder, hip, hand, heel

TAME & Plantar Fasciitis Embolization

Transcatheter arterial micro-embolization (TAME) applies the GAE approach to other painful joints and tendons. Evidence is early, so we select patients carefully.

Refer when

✓Pain for 3 to 6+ months despite PT, NSAIDs and at least one injection
✓Shoulder: adhesive capsulitis or rotator cuff tendinopathy
✓Hip: OA pain in patients not ready for, or not eligible for, replacement
✓Hand: finger joint or thumb-base OA
✓Heel: plantar fasciitis despite stretching, orthotics and injection
✓Patients who want to avoid or delay surgery

Likely not a fit

–Full-thickness tears or problems that need surgery
–Active infection or uncorrectable coagulopathy
–Advanced CKD or severe contrast allergy
–Raynaud’s, scleroderma or digital ischemia (hand)

What we offer

ShoulderAdhesive capsulitis, rotator cuff tendinopathy, shoulder OA
HipOsteoarthritis pain (investigational)
Hand & wristFinger and thumb-base OA, chronic wrist pain
Heel (PFE)Plantar fasciitis that has not responded to standard care
SettingOutpatient, small wrist or groin puncture; home the same day

Send with the referral

•Imaging of the area (X-ray, MRI or ultrasound)
•Treatment history, including injections and PT
•Medication list, especially anticoagulants

Evidence at a glance

EarlyEvidence is mostly from uncontrolled case series. A 2021 systematic review (19 studies, 394 patients) found no randomized trials.1
92Patients in a retrospective hand OA series (temporary agent, single arm); pain improved at 12 months, no major adverse events.2
25Patients in a single-arm adhesive capsulitis series; most reported less night pain within a month.3
LimitedPlantar fasciitis data come from small retrospective and pilot studies.4
Shared decisionsWe review the evidence and alternatives with every patient and report outcomes to you. Embolic agents are used off-label for joint pain; results vary. Coverage varies.

1. Hindsø L et al. Cardiovasc Intervent Radiol 2021;44:1699-1708. 2. Kubo T, Miyazaki K et al. Cardiovasc Intervent Radiol 2023;46:1375-82. 3. Okuno Y et al. TAE for adhesive capsulitis resistant to conservative treatment. J Vasc Interv Radiol 2017. 4. Tuna HS et al. Diagnostics 2026;16:2217.
