Joint & tendon pain guide

Areas We Treat

Embolization can be used for pain in many joints and tendons. The procedure name depends on the area treated.

1

Shoulder

Frozen shoulder, rotator cuff tendon problems and shoulder arthritis

TAME

2

Hip

Arthritis pain in people not ready for, or not able to have, hip replacement

TAME

3

Knee

Mild to moderate osteoarthritis, and some cases of pain after knee replacement

GAE

4

Hand & thumb

Arthritis in the finger joints and at the base of the thumb

TAME

5

Wrist

Arthritis and long-lasting wrist pain

TAME

6

Ankle

Arthritis and long-lasting ankle pain

TAME

7

Heel

Plantar fasciitis that hasn’t improved with stretching, shoes or injections

PFE

8

Lower back

Certain types of long-lasting lower back pain; ask whether you may be a candidate

Ask your specialist

GAE: genicular artery embolization. TAME: transcatheter arterial micro-embolization. PFE: plantar fasciitis embolization. Not every patient is a candidate. Your specialist will review your symptoms and imaging to decide whether a procedure may help.
