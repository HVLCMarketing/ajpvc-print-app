Joint & tendon pain guide

Preparing for Your Procedure

Our team will give you instructions before your procedure. Follow them carefully, and call us with any questions.

Before Your Procedure

Tell us about all the medicines you take, including vitamins and supplements. You may be asked to stop blood thinners or some diabetes medicines for a short time. Don’t stop any medicine unless your doctor tells you to.
Tell us if you are allergic to contrast dye, iodine or any medicines.
You may need blood tests to check your kidney function and blood clotting.
Don’t eat or drink before your procedure for as long as our staff tells you.
Arrange for an adult to drive you home and stay with you overnight.

On the Day

Wear loose, comfortable clothing and leave jewelry at home.
Bring your ID, insurance card and list of medicines.
An IV line will be placed for fluids and relaxing medicine.
You may be asked to point to where it hurts most. This helps guide treatment.
After the procedure, you’ll rest for a few hours while we check the puncture site.

We Check Your Insurance First

Coverage for joint embolization varies, and some plans consider it investigational. We verify your benefits and explain any costs before your procedure is scheduled.
