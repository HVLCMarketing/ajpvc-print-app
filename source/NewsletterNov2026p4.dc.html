Persistent heel pain: Plantar fascia embolization

Plantar fascia embolization (PFE) targets abnormal small vessels associated with chronic plantar fasciitis. It may be considered for selected patients whose heel pain continues despite appropriate conservative care. This is an emerging treatment; an evaluation should include its uncertainties and other options.

Internal hemorrhoids: Hemorrhoidal artery embolization

Hemorrhoidal artery embolization (HAE) reduces blood supply to internal hemorrhoidal tissue and may help selected patients, particularly those with bleeding. It does not treat every type of hemorrhoid. Rectal bleeding needs an evaluation rather than an assumption about its cause.

November reminder: Do not overlook your feet

November is National Diabetes Month. Diabetes can reduce feeling and blood flow in the feet, so a wound may be serious even when it is not painful. Check your feet daily for cuts, blisters, redness, swelling, or sores. Contact your healthcare team promptly about a new wound, a sore that is not healing, or signs of infection.
