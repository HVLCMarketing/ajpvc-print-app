Joint & tendon pain guide

Self-Care for Your Joints

What you do every day matters. Self-care can ease pain on its own and help you get the most from any treatment.

Stay Active

Low-impact activities like walking, swimming and cycling keep joints moving without extra strain. Start slowly and build up.

Manage Your Weight

Extra weight adds pressure to the hips, knees and feet. Even a small weight loss can ease that pressure.

Build Strength

Strong muscles support and protect your joints. A physical therapist can show you exercises that are safe for you.

Use Heat and Cold

Heat can loosen stiff joints. Cold can calm swelling after activity. Use either for 15 to 20 minutes at a time, with a cloth between your skin and the pack.

Protect Your Joints

Wear supportive shoes, use tools with large grips and pace yourself. For heel pain, stretch your calf and the bottom of your foot before your first steps in the morning.

Track Your Progress

Rate your pain each week and note what you were able to do. Bring this to your follow-up visits.
