Breaking the Pain Cycle

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • Pain leads to less activity, which causes muscle weakness and loss of conditioning, making stairs, chairs and distance harder and reducing activity further.
  • The cycle does not mean every increase in pain reflects worsening structural damage; pain, movement and physical capacity influence one another.
  • The aim is not to eliminate all discomfort first, but to reduce symptoms enough to allow safe, sustainable activity and better function.

Treatment options

  • Education, exercise and physical therapy
  • Weight management, medications and assistive devices
  • GAE or orthopedic care when indicated

Ask your patient

  • What has your knee pain stopped you from doing?
  • How far can you walk, and how are stairs and chairs?
  • Has the pain affected your sleep or mood?

Refer when

  • Rapid loss of function or inability to bear weight.
  • Persistent low mood or hopelessness: discuss support and treatment.
  • Hot, swollen joint with fever: urgent evaluation for infection.
