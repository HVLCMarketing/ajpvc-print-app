Inside the Joint Lining: Extra Vessels and Nerves

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • Inflamed synovium signals new vessel growth; new vessels may let in more inflammatory cells, sustaining a cycle.
  • Sensory nerves may grow alongside new vessels, and inflammation can make nerve endings more sensitive.
  • This is one possible contributor to OA pain; mechanisms remain under investigation and sham-controlled trials are mixed.

Treatment options

  • Foundation care: exercise, physical therapy, appropriate medications
  • Steroid injection to reduce inflammation short term
  • GAE to reduce flow to selected abnormal vessels

Ask your patient

  • Does your knee swell or feel warm?
  • Where is your single most painful spot?
  • Is the pain worse at night or at rest?

Refer when

  • Persistent symptomatic knee OA despite appropriate care: discuss GAE evaluation.
  • Suspected rheumatoid or psoriatic arthritis: rheumatology — GAE is not an established first-line treatment.
