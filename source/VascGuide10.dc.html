Varicose Veins & Venous Insufficiency

Part 2 · Veins

When vein valves leak, blood pools in the legs. Over time, this can cause visible veins, swelling, skin changes and even open sores.

Spider veins

Small red or purple veins near the skin

Varicose veins

Skin changes

›

Venous ulcer

An open sore, often near the ankle

Not everyone moves through every stage. Many people stay at one stage for years.

Common Symptoms

  • Aching, heavy or tired legs
  • Swelling in the ankles or lower legs
  • Itching or burning skin
  • Leg cramps or restless legs at night
  • Symptoms that feel better when you raise your legs

Risk Factors

  • A family history of vein problems
  • Pregnancy
  • Getting older
  • Long periods of standing or sitting
  • Extra weight
  • A past blood clot

Spider Veins

Spider veins are often a cosmetic concern, and treatment may not be covered by insurance. Sometimes they are a sign of a leaking vein underneath, so an ultrasound can help.
