Where will my procedure take place?

The satellite offices provide consultations and follow-up visits. Procedures are performed at Heart Vascular & Leg Center, 5020 Commerce Drive, Bakersfield, CA 93309. Your care team will confirm the location for each appointment.

What should I bring?

Bring your medication list, insurance information, relevant imaging or test reports, and a brief list of treatments you have already tried. Write down the questions you most want answered.

Questions about insurance? Ask our team to review your benefits, referral requirements, authorization needs, and anticipated costs. Coverage depends on the procedure and your plan.
