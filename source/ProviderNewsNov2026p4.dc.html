Beyond joint-pain embolization

Available through Heart Vascular & Leg Center; service location and eligibility are confirmed individually.

Plantar fascia embolization (PFE)

Evaluation for selected patients with established plantar fasciitis refractory to appropriate conservative care. Published studies include small cohorts; counsel regarding the developing evidence and alternatives.

Peripheral nerve stimulation (PNS)

Targeted electrical stimulation to modulate pain signaling in selected chronic pain conditions. Document pain distribution, the suspected nerve or pain generator, prior treatment response, and functional impact. PNS is not an embolization procedure.

Hemorrhoidal artery embolization (HAE)

Evaluation for selected symptomatic internal hemorrhoids, particularly persistent bleeding. Establish the source of bleeding and include relevant GI or colorectal workup; HAE does not replace evaluation of other causes of rectal bleeding.

November focus: Diabetes, perfusion, and foot health

National Diabetes Month is a reminder to assess both sensation and circulation when evaluating foot concerns. Neuropathy can reduce protective sensation, while impaired blood flow can complicate healing. A nonhealing wound warrants assessment of its vascular contribution, not wound treatment in isolation. Include wound history, examination findings, prior testing, and urgency in the referral.

Coverage and authorization are procedure- and plan-specific. A referral requests an evaluation; it does not establish procedural eligibility or insurance coverage.
