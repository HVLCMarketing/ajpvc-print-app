Pocket guide for providers

When to Refer

Knee, hip, shoulder, hand or heel pain for 3+ months despite PT, NSAIDs and an injection; not ready for surgery→ Joint embolization consult (GAE, TAME, PFE)
Leg pain with walking, or ABI 0.90 or lower→ PAD evaluation and arterial testing
UrgentRest pain, a nonhealing foot or leg wound, or gangrene→ Priority limb-salvage visit
Symptomatic varicose veins, venous swelling, skin changes or a venous ulcer→ Venous reflux duplex and vein treatment
Same daySuspected DVT→ Same-day DVT rule-out (most major plans)
Unexplained left-leg swelling, post-thrombotic symptoms, or a retrievable IVC filter→ Deep venous evaluation or filter removal
Hard cannulation, prolonged bleeding, arm swelling or a clotted dialysis access (call same day)→ Fistulagram and angioplasty
Evaluated TIA with 50–99% carotid stenosis, or asymptomatic stenosis of 70%+→ Carotid evaluation
