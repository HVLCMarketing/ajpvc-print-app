Referring provider guide

Arterial Care & Limb Salvage

Peripheral artery disease is common and often silent. Earlier diagnosis protects limbs and flags cardiovascular risk.

Who to Refer

✓Claudication, or an ABI of 0.90 or lower
✓Urgent: rest pain, a nonhealing wound or gangrene
✓Diabetic foot wounds with weak pulses
✓Dialysis access dysfunction
✓Carotid stenosis after an evaluated TIA

Guideline Pearls

✓A normal ABI does not rule out CLTI; at least 25% have a normal or borderline ABI.1
✓Use a toe-brachial index when the ABI is above 1.40.1
✓Structured exercise for claudication, antiplatelet therapy and a high-intensity statin are Class 1.1
✓Symptomatic carotid stenosis: revascularization favored within 14 days.2

Testing

ABI and exercise ABI, skin perfusion pressure, arterial, carotid, renal and aortic duplex

Treatment

Angiography, angioplasty, stenting, atherectomy, fistulagram; surgical referral when bypass is better

Wound healing

Coordinated wound care and hyperbaric oxygen therapy when indicated
