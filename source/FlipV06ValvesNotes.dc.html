Venous insufficiency

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • One-way valves fail, so blood refluxes and pools (CEAP C2–C6).
  • Symptoms: heaviness, aching, swelling, skin changes, ulcers.
  • A reflux duplex ultrasound confirms it.

Treatment options

  • Compression stockings, elevation, walking
  • Vein closure: RFA, VenaSeal, Varithena
  • Phlebectomy and sclerotherapy

Ask your patient

  • Do your legs feel heavy or ache by the end of the day?
  • Any swelling, skin darkening or sores?

Refer when

  • Symptomatic varicose veins or venous swelling
  • Early: any venous ulcer
