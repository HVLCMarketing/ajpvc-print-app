Send to the ED, not a referral

!Sudden cold, pale, painful or numb limb (acute limb ischemia)
!Suspected PE: sudden shortness of breath, chest pain, coughing up blood
!Massive leg swelling with a blue or pale limb
!Foot wound with spreading redness, pus, wet gangrene or fever
!Access bleeding that won’t stop, or an aneurysm with thin or broken skin
!Stroke symptoms: call 911; a new, unevaluated TIA
