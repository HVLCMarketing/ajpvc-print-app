We find and treat problems in arteries and veins throughout the body, especially the legs and feet.

Peripheral artery disease

Narrowed leg arteries that cause pain with walking

Angioplasty · Stent · Atherectomy

5

Varicose & spider veins

Bulging, aching or visible veins in the legs

RFA · VenaSeal · Varithena · Phlebectomy

6

Blood clots (DVT)

A clot in a deep vein, usually in the leg

Same-day ultrasound · Treatment

7

Swelling & skin changes

Heavy, swollen legs; darkened or thickened skin

Vein testing · Vein treatment

8

Nonhealing wounds

Sores on the legs or feet that won’t heal

Circulation care · Wound care · HBOT

RFA: radiofrequency ablation. HBOT: hyperbaric oxygen therapy. Your specialist will recommend tests and treatment based on your symptoms and exam.
