Is embolization right for me?

You may be a candidate if you:

  • Have had joint or lower back pain for 3 months or more
  • Have arthritis or joint inflammation confirmed by X-ray or other imaging
  • Haven't found relief with medication, physical therapy or injections
  • Aren't ready for, can't have, or want to delay joint replacement

It may not be right if you have

  • An active infection
  • A cancer in or near the joint
  • Severe arthritis better treated with joint replacement

Only your specialist can decide whether you're a candidate.
