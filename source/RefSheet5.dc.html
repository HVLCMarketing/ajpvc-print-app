Venous disease

DVT, Leg Swelling & Venous Obstruction

Same-day DVT rule-out, deep venous care and IVC filter retrieval.

Refer when

✓Suspected DVT: same-day duplex rule-out (most major plans; call to confirm)
✓Extensive iliofemoral DVT
✓Unexplained or recurrent left-leg swelling (possible iliac vein compression)
✓Post-thrombotic symptoms: chronic swelling, heaviness, skin changes, ulcers
✓A retrievable IVC filter that is still in place

Send to the ED instead

!Suspected PE (sudden shortness of breath, chest pain, coughing up blood)
!Massive swelling with a blue or pale limb
