Knee pain and GAE

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • Osteoarthritis wears cartilage and inflames the synovium.
  • Inflamed synovium grows abnormal small vessels and nerve fibers that drive pain.
  • GAE delivers tiny particles through a microcatheter to reduce that blood flow.

Treatment options

  • Self-care, weight management, PT, NSAIDs
  • Injections (steroid, hyaluronic acid)
  • GAE: outpatient, small puncture, home the same day
  • Knee replacement for end-stage disease

Ask your patient

  • How long has the pain lasted?
  • What has helped, and what hasn’t?
  • Are you hoping to avoid or delay surgery?

Refer when

  • Pain 3+ months despite PT, NSAIDs and/or injections
  • Mild to moderate OA (KL 1–3)
  • Not a replacement candidate, or wants to delay surgery
