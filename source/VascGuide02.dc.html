Arterial & venous guide

Understanding Your Circulation

Healthy blood flow keeps your legs strong, your skin healthy and your wounds healing. This booklet explains common artery and vein problems, how they are found and the treatments available.

Arteries

Arteries carry oxygen-rich blood away from the heart to every part of your body, including your legs and feet.

Veins

Veins carry blood back to the heart. In the legs, veins work against gravity, helped by your calf muscles and tiny one-way valves.

Who Is at Risk?

  • Adults over 50
  • People who smoke, or used to
  • People with diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol
  • A family history of artery or vein disease
  • For vein problems: pregnancy, jobs with long standing or sitting, extra weight

When Blood Flow Goes Wrong

Narrowed arteries can starve muscles and skin of oxygen. Weak or blocked veins let blood pool in the legs. Both can cause pain, swelling, skin changes and wounds that won’t heal, and both can often be treated with minimally invasive procedures.
