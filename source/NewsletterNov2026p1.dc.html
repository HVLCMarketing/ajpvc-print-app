A Thanksgiving message from Dr. Kumar

Dear Patients and Families,

As Thanksgiving approaches, I want to thank you for allowing our team to be part of your care. Your questions, your goals, and the trust you place in us matter.

This season, we are pleased to announce new Advanced Joint Pain & Vascular Center satellite locations for consultations and follow-up visits. Our goal is to make it easier to take that first step and stay connected to your care team.

When you come in, tell us more than where it hurts. Tell us what you miss doing. Is it walking with your family, sleeping comfortably, reaching a shelf, or preparing a meal? Those details help us understand what matters to you.

You do not need to choose a procedure before asking for help. Start with a conversation. We will discuss your concerns, review your options, and help you understand the next step.

Wishing you and your family a meaningful Thanksgiving season.

With appreciation,
Dr. Vinod Kumar · Heart Vascular & Leg Center
