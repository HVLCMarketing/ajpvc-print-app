Arterial & venous guide

Angiogram, Angioplasty & Stenting

These procedures find and open narrowed arteries from the inside, through a small puncture. No large incision is needed.

Angiogram

The catheter usually goes in through a small puncture in the groin or wrist, sometimes the foot.

Angioplasty

Some balloons are coated with medicine to help keep the artery open longer.

Stenting

You’ll likely take an antiplatelet medicine afterward, such as aspirin or clopidogrel (Plavix). Don’t stop it without talking to your specialist. Stopping early can cause the stent to clot.
