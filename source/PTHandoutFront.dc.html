Still hurting after physical therapy?

Ongoing knee, hip, shoulder or other joint pain may have more than one cause. A second opinion can show whether another treatment option, including a minimally invasive procedure, may be right for you. Not every patient is a candidate.

Two ways to get started

Your insurance plan decides which one applies to you. If you are not sure, call us and we will check.

My plan requires a referral

1

Bring this sheet to your primary care provider (PCP).

2

Ask for a referral to Advanced Joint Pain & Vascular Center. The back page tells your PCP what we need.

3

Their office sends it by fax or secure email. We then call you to schedule and verify your insurance.

My plan does not require a referral

1

Call or scan to request an appointment. No referral needed.

2

We verify your insurance before your visit, so there are no surprises.

3

Bring your imaging, medication list and notes from physical therapy.
