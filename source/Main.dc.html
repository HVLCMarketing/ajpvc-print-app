What is joint embolization?

Arthritis and other chronic joint conditions can bring extra blood vessels and inflammation to the tissue around a joint. Embolization uses a thin catheter and X-ray guidance to reduce blood flow to those inflamed areas. In the knee, it's called genicular artery embolization (GAE).

It is an option for some people whose pain hasn't improved with conservative care. Research is ongoing, results vary, and like any procedure it has risks. Your specialist will walk you through the benefits, risks and alternatives.
