Arterial & venous guide

Atherectomy & Other Arterial Care

Some blockages need more than a balloon. We also care for arteries in the neck, dialysis access and wounds caused by poor circulation.

Atherectomy

A catheter with a tiny cutting, sanding or laser tip removes plaque from inside the artery. It’s often combined with angioplasty, especially for hard, calcified plaque.

Carotid artery care

The carotid arteries in your neck supply the brain. Narrowing raises the risk of stroke. We check them with ultrasound, and an angiogram can give a more detailed look.

Dialysis access

Fistulas and grafts can narrow over time. A fistulagram finds the problem, and angioplasty or a stent can restore flow so dialysis can continue.

Wound care & HBOT

Wounds heal slowly when blood flow is poor. We test circulation, treat blockages and provide wound care. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy may help certain hard-to-heal wounds.

!

Don’t Wait on a Foot Wound

If you have a sore on your foot or leg that isn’t healing, or a toe that turns dark, blue or black, call us right away. Finding and treating poor blood flow early can lower the risk of serious infection and amputation.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT)

You rest in a special chamber and breathe pure oxygen at higher pressure. This raises the oxygen in your blood, which may help some wounds heal. Treatments are usually given in a series of sessions. Your care team will tell you whether it fits your wound and what your insurance covers.
