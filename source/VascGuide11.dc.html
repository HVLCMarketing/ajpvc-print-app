Closing the Faulty Vein

The most common treatment for venous reflux is to close the leaking vein from the inside. These are outpatient procedures done with ultrasound guidance.

TreatmentHow it worksWhat you’ll feelAfterward
Radiofrequency ablation (RFA)Heat from a thin catheter seals the vein.Numbing medicine is injected along the vein.Compression stockings are usually worn for a short time.
VenaSealA medical adhesive seals the vein closed.Usually only one numbing injection.Compression is often not needed.
VarithenaA prescription foam is injected to close the vein.No incision; usually a small needle stick.Compression stockings are worn afterward.
Mechanochemical ablation (MOCA)A rotating wire and a medicine close the vein, without heat.Little numbing medicine is needed.Compression is usually worn afterward.

Your specialist will recommend the best option based on your veins, your ultrasound and your insurance coverage.
