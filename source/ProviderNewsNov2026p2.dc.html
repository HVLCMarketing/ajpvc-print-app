Procedure location remains Bakersfield

Procedures are performed at Heart Vascular & Leg Center, 5020 Commerce Drive, Bakersfield, CA 93309. Satellite consultation access should not be interpreted as procedure availability at each office.

Send the clinical question, not just a procedure request

Include the reason for referral, suspected diagnosis, symptom duration, affected side or joint, functional limitations, relevant imaging, prior treatments and response, medication and allergy lists, and significant comorbidities. Add vascular findings, wound documentation, and clinical urgency when relevant.

For TAME evaluation, specify hip, shoulder, or hand and the affected side or joints. Include patient contact and insurance information, the preferred satellite city, and a contact for clinical questions.

Refer for an evaluation

Use Refer a Patient at heartandleg.com. Urgent concerns require direct clinical contact; emergencies require emergency evaluation.
