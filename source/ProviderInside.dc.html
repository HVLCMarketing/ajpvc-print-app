Who to refer

  • Chronic pain (3+ months) in the knee, hip, shoulder, hand, wrist, ankle, lower back or heel
  • Inadequate relief from NSAIDs, PT and/or injections
  • Not ready for, ineligible for, or wanting to delay surgery
  • Knee: mild-to-moderate OA (KL 1–3), or pain after knee replacement
  • Shoulder: e.g., frozen shoulder or rotator cuff tendinopathy
  • Hip: arthritis-related pain not yet ready for replacement

Likely not a fit

  • Severe end-stage arthritis better suited to joint replacement
  • Active infection or uncorrectable coagulopathy
  • Severe contrast allergy, advanced CKD, or significant PAD in the limb

What the evidence shows

Knee (GAE) is the most studied: cohort studies report meaningful pain improvement, but two small sham-controlled RCTs (n=58 and 59) did not show significant superiority over sham; larger trials are under way. Evidence for shoulder, hip and other joints is earlier-stage. We track outcomes on every patient and share them with you.
