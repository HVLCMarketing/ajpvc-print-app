Arterial & venous guide

Risks & When to Call

These are minimally invasive procedures, but every procedure has risks. Your specialist will talk with you about them before you decide.

Artery Procedures

  • Bleeding or bruising at the puncture site
  • Infection at the puncture site
  • Injury to the artery
  • Reaction to contrast dye
  • Strain on the kidneys from contrast dye
  • The artery narrowing again over time, which may need another procedure
  • Rarely, a blood clot or blockage

Vein Procedures

  • Bruising, tenderness and tightness
  • Skin discoloration along the vein
  • Numbness or tingling in a small patch of skin
  • A tender, firm lump along the vein (phlebitis)
  • Infection at a puncture or incision site
  • Rarely, nerve irritation or a skin burn
  • Rarely, a blood clot in a deep vein or an allergic reaction
!

Call Us Right Away

Call 661-324-4100 if you have:

  • Bleeding or swelling at the puncture site
  • A lump at the site that grows or pulses
  • Fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher
  • New or worse calf pain or leg swelling
  • A wound that looks worse, red or drains

Call 911 if you have

Heavy bleeding that won’t stop with firm pressure (lie down and keep pressing on the site until help arrives); a leg that suddenly turns cold, pale, numb or very painful; chest pain or sudden shortness of breath; or signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking).

For urgent concerns after hours, call 661-324-4100.
