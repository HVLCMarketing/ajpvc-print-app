Arterial & venous guide

Your Treatment Plan

After testing, you and your specialist will make a plan together. Most plans combine healthy habits with treatment.

Treatment Options

1Healthy habitsA walking program, quitting smoking, managing weight
2Compression stockingsFor vein problems and leg swelling
3MedicinesTo prevent clots and control cholesterol, blood pressure and diabetes
4Wound careSpecial dressings and, for some wounds, hyperbaric oxygen
5Minimally invasive proceduresDone through a small puncture, usually outpatient
6SurgerySuch as bypass, when other options aren’t right

Good to Know

Insurance and compression stockings. Many plans require you to wear compression stockings for several weeks before they cover a vein procedure. Our team will tell you what your plan requires.

Walking helps. For PAD, a regular walking program can increase how far you can walk without pain. Ask about a supervised exercise program, which many plans, including Medicare, cover.

Smoking matters. Quitting is one of the most important things you can do for your arteries.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor

  • What is causing my symptoms?
  • What are my treatment options?
  • What are the risks for someone like me?
  • How long is recovery?
  • Will my insurance cover it, and what will I pay?
