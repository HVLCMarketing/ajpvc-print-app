A message from Dr. Kumar

Dear Colleagues,

A referral for persistent pain is rarely about a pain score alone. It may be about walking tolerance, disrupted sleep, the ability to work, or declining independence. Those are the outcomes we want at the center of treatment discussions.

This November, we are pleased to announce seven Advanced Joint Pain & Vascular Center satellite locations for consultation and follow-up. Our goal is to make specialty evaluation easier to access while keeping the diagnosis, previous treatment, and patient priorities central to each decision.

In this issue, we outline referral considerations for genicular artery embolization and transcatheter arterial microembolization, alongside our broader arterial, venous, wound, cardiac, and pain services. A referral can begin with a clinical question; it does not need to begin with a predetermined procedure.

Thank you for the trust you place in our team and for your partnership in caring for our communities. We are grateful for the opportunity to work with you.

With appreciation,
Dr. Vinod Kumar · Heart Vascular & Leg Center
