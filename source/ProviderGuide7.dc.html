How Referral Works

1

Send the order form

Fax it to 661-324-4600 or send it securely to referrals@heartandleg.com, with recent notes, imaging and labs.

2

Call for urgent needs

For same-day DVT rule-out, rest pain or a nonhealing wound, call the referral coordinator at 661-459-9010.

3

We reach your patient

Our team schedules the visit and verifies benefits, including prior authorization when needed.

4

You hear back

You receive our consult note and test results, and updates after any procedure.

What Helps Us Help Your Patient

✓Reason for referral and key history
✓Recent imaging and reports (X-ray, MRI, duplex, ABI)
✓Medication list, especially anticoagulants
✓Recent kidney function for procedures that use contrast
✓Insurance information
