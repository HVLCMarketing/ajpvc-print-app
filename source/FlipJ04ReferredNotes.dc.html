Where Pain Starts Isn’t Always Where It Hurts

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • Hip disease and lumbar spine problems can produce pain felt around the knee, thigh or buttock.
  • Knee-directed treatment may not help if the main pain source is elsewhere.
  • GAE treats pain arising from the knee itself; it cannot fix pain coming from the hip or spine.

Treatment options

  • Treat each pain generator on its own terms
  • Knee treatments, including GAE, when the knee is the main source
  • Separate evaluation and care for hip or back pain

Ask your patient

  • Does the pain spread into your thigh, buttock or lower back?
  • Do you have numbness, tingling or weakness?
  • Are several joints affected, or have you had fever?

Refer when

  • Back pain with new bladder or bowel dysfunction or saddle numbness: emergency.
  • New weakness, numbness, limb color or temperature change, or absent pulses.
  • Persistent swelling of small hand or foot joints: rheumatologic evaluation.
