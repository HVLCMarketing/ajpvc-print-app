Provider talking points · 03

Angiogram, angioplasty and stenting

Patient sees the facing page

Explain it simply

  • Done through a small puncture, usually the groin or wrist.
  • Often outpatient; rest a few hours while the site is checked.
  • Atherectomy removes plaque when needed.

Treatment options

  • Angioplasty, drug-coated balloons
  • Stents
  • Atherectomy
  • Surgical bypass when it’s the better option

Ask your patient

  • Any allergy to contrast dye?
  • Kidney problems?
  • Can you take antiplatelet medicine?

Refer when

  • Lifestyle-limiting claudication or limb-threatening ischemia
