Print-shop PDF: choose Save as PDF, margins None, background graphics on. Text and shapes stay vector with fonts embedded; photos stay at their original resolution. The page is the trim size plus 1/8 in bleed and a slug with crop marks. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF (or your printer), set margins to None, and turn on Background graphics. For two-sided printing choose Flip on long edge; the back side is already turned to match. Office printers can't print to the paper edge: for a full-color edge, tick Edge-to-edge on an office printer, print on 11 × 17, and trim at the marks.
